Nostalgic, quick, and undeniably comforting, **Toast Soldiers** take us straight back to childhood weekend mornings. By using rich, egg-washed French brioche instead of standard white sandwich bread, each baton becomes crunchy like a churro on the outside while remaining tender and pillowy on the inside.
