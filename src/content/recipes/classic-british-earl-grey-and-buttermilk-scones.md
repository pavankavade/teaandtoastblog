There is no British ritual more sacred than the afternoon cream tea. Done properly, a fresh scone should pull apart cleanly horizontally into two halves with no knife required, revealing a steamy, tender crumb ready to welcome spoonfuls of velvety clotted cream and jewel-bright jam.

### The Great Debate: Jam First or Cream First?
In **Devon**, tradition dictates that clotted cream is spread first, acting like butter, followed by a dollop of strawberry jam. In **Cornwall**, strawberry jam is spread first directly on the warm scone, crowned with a generous spoonful of thick clotted cream.

Whichever camp you belong to, the foundation must be a warm, freshly baked scone paired with a properly brewed teapot of whole-leaf Darjeeling or English Breakfast.
