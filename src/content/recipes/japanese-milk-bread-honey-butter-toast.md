If you have ever visited a kissaten (traditional coffee house) in Tokyo or Kyoto, you know the quiet magic of thick-cut honey butter toast (Ogura or Hachimitsu butter toast). 

Unlike Western sandwich bread, **Shokupan** is sliced into generous two-inch slabs. Scoring the bread allows melted butter and warm floral honey to seep deep into the crumb, turning the interior into a pillowy, custardy cloud while the exterior develops a shatteringly crisp, caramelized crust.
