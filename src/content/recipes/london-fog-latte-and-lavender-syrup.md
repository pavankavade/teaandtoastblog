Created in Vancouver, British Columbia in the late 1990s, the **London Fog** is the tea lover’s answer to the cappuccino. 

When cold rain taps against the windowpane, there is no sensory comfort quite like cradling a warm ceramic mug brimming with frothy, vanilla-laced Earl Grey. The essential oil of bergamot (a fragrant Italian citrus fruit) combined with sweet vanilla and subtle herbal lavender creates a warm, floral hug.
