Avocado toast is ubiquitous on modern café menus, but far too often it arrives as a soggy slice of bland bread topped with lukewarm, under-seasoned puree.

At **Tea & Toast**, our philosophy is that everyday breakfast staples deserve culinary reverence. When you dial in three specific elements—**bread temperature**, **acid balance**, and **textural contrast**—this humble dish transforms into a masterpiece.

### The Anatomy of Great Avocado Toast

1. **The Bread Foundation**: Soft sandwich bread wilts under the weight of avocado. You need a sourdough with an open crumb, blistered crust, and subtle lactic tang. Searing the bread in olive oil creates a barrier that keeps the crumb crunchy even 10 minutes into breakfast.
2. **The Acid Factor**: Avocado is packed with rich, velvety oleic acids. Without enough citrus to brighten it, each bite feels heavy. We use both lime juice and a zest of lemon to wake up the palate.
3. **The 6.5-Minute Egg**: A hard-boiled egg is too dry, while a runny sunny-side-up egg can spill everywhere. A soft-boiled jammy egg—with firm whites and a custard-like, glowing yolk—sits comfortably on the toast and enriches the chili crunch.

### Why We Pair It with Earl Grey

Black tea with oil of bergamot is the sommelier's secret for savory avocado dishes. The astringency of the tea leaves cleanses the palate of richness, while the aromatic bergamot citrus resonates with the lime in the avocado mash.
